MoSCoW Prioritisation

Must / Should / Could / Won’t — a simple, shared language for ranking scope.

Purpose

MoSCoW helps teams and stakeholders agree what is essential versus nice-to-have within a fixed timebox or budget.

  • Must have — non-negotiable for success
  • Should have — important but not critical
  • Could have — desirable if capacity remains
  • Won’t have (this time) — explicitly deferred
