07 · Quality

Acceptance Criteria

Clear, testable conditions that determine when a requirement or story is complete.

Purpose

Well-written acceptance criteria remove ambiguity and form the basis for testing and sign-off. They answer: “How do we know this is done?”

  • Given / When / Then (or bullet-style) criteria
  • Positive & negative scenarios
  • Edge cases and non-functional checks
  • Definition of Done alignment
  • Traceability back to requirements / stories

Workspace
