Stakeholder Analysis

Identifies who is affected, their influence and interest, and how to engage them.

Purpose

Understanding stakeholders reduces resistance and improves decision quality. Typical outputs include power/interest grids and engagement plans.

  • Stakeholder register (roles, concerns, influence)
  • Power / Interest (or Influence / Impact) matrix
  • Engagement strategy per group
  • Communication cadence & channels
  • Risks related to stakeholder dynamics

Workspace

Build stakeholder matrices, RACI charts, and engagement plans here. Visual language mirrors the parent page.
