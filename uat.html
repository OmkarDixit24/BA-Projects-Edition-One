09 · Validation

User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

Validates that the delivered solution meets business needs in a real-world context.

Purpose

UAT is the final business checkpoint before go-live. It confirms that acceptance criteria are met and that the solution is fit for purpose.

  • UAT plan & entry/exit criteria
  • Test scenarios linked to requirements / stories
  • Participant roles & environment
  • Defect logging & severity
  • Sign-off and residual risk

Workspace

Store UAT scripts, results, and sign-off evidence here. Same visual structure and CSS language as the mind-map parent.
